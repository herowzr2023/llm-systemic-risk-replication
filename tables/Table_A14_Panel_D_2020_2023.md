Posts are retained archived records after basic title-validity screening, without URL deduplication.

Active calendar days contain at least one retained record. Zero-post calendar days contain none. The denominator is the intersection of the subperiod and the global valid-publication-date archive range (1994-12-31 to 2023-02-01). This panel counts 1,128 calendar days, 2020-01-01 to 2023-02-01, inclusive. Active-day % equals 100 times active days divided by calendar days counted. These calendar-day statistics differ from the trading-day windows used in forecasting.

Distinct author labels count nonempty display names within each bank and subperiod. Shared labels may represent different accounts; counts are not additive across periods.

View and comment summaries are record-level 25th, 50th and 75th percentiles of available fields, including valid zeros. The public inputs provide bank/subperiod counts, period and archive date bounds, separate value-frequency distributions for views and comments, and archive inventory totals. Percentile ranks are located in cumulative frequencies and linearly interpolated at position (n − 1)p in the ordered distribution. NA denotes an unavailable statistic. These archival fields are descriptive and are not used in the forecasting predictors.
